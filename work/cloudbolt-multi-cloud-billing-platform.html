Scaling a Multi-Cloud Billing Platform for CSPs and MSPs

By Navin Kumar · Lead Software Engineer · Jun 2022 – Present

Leading the team behind a Ruby on Rails platform that lets cloud service providers and managed service providers manage multiple public clouds — and their billing — from one place.

Short version: CloudBolt's platform gives CSPs and MSPs a single place to manage various public clouds, including the billing side of that business. I lead the engineering team building it, having grown into that role from hands-on senior engineering on the same product.

The context

Cloud service providers and managed service providers who resell or manage public cloud infrastructure for their own customers face a specific problem: doing that across multiple clouds means multiple consoles, multiple billing models, and multiple places for something to go wrong. CloudBolt's platform exists to bring that under one roof — managing various public clouds, and the billing that goes with them, from a single system.

What I've done

I joined as a Senior Software Engineer, designing scalable REST APIs and backend services for high-volume enterprise workloads and automating infrastructure provisioning with AWS CloudFormation. I've since grown into Lead Software Engineer, where I now lead a team of 5+ engineers delivering this platform to 10,000+ enterprise users.

The biggest structural change I've driven is a monolith-to-microservices migration — reducing technical debt and improving scalability on a codebase that has to keep running reliably for paying enterprise customers while it's being re-architected underneath them. Alongside that, I've improved deployment frequency from bi-weekly to weekly releases through CI/CD work, cut response times by roughly 30% through caching and database tuning, and maintained 99.9%+ uptime through more resilient architecture and better monitoring.

The leadership side

Leading the team on this platform means the job isn't only technical anymore. I partner with product and leadership to keep the engineering roadmap aligned with what CSP and MSP customers actually need from their billing and cloud-management workflows, and I mentor engineers on the team — the same kind of hands-on technical judgment I built up as a senior engineer, now multiplied across other people's work as well as my own.

Why this matters if you're hiring: this is the throughline across my career — from sole-owner of a warehouse system to leading a 5-engineer team on enterprise infrastructure. The scale changes; the habit of owning outcomes, not just tickets, doesn't.
